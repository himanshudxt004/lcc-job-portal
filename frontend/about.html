Bridging the gap between education and employability.

LCC was born with one simple vision — to turn the dreams of millions of young Indians into employable, industry-ready careers.

Who We Are
An education-driven organisation, founded by a spirited blend of young minds and seasoned veterans.

Across India, we noticed a familiar story: bright graduates stepping into the job market, yet struggling to match the expectations of employers. The gap wasn't talent — it was training. It was exposure. It was industry readiness.

So we decided to change the story. LCC was created to ensure every student — whether from a metro or a small town — gets access to affordable, practical and meaningful upskilling. We train students while they are still in college, helping them build real-world skills, confidence and clarity about their career path.

  • Founders from Banking & NBFCs, Executive Recruitment, and Legal Services
  • Programs designed with industry experts — not just academic theorists
  • Pan-India presence covering metros and tier-2/3 towns equally
  • Affordable upskilling, accessible to every student
